# References

The links below identify the source and, when a version matters, the version used. The [related-work comparison](RELATED_WORK.md) separates inherited ingredients from the finite-rate recycling result. Repository snapshots are cited as mathematical notes, not as peer-reviewed publications.

## Woods2022

Mischa P. Woods, Ralph Silva, Gilles Pütz, Sandra Stupar, and Renato Renner, **“Quantum Clocks are More Accurate Than Classical Ones,”** *PRX Quantum* **3**, 010319 (2022).

- [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.3.010319).
- [arXiv:1806.00491v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.00491v3), revised 24 February 2022; the arXiv title is “Quantum clocks are more precise than classical ones.”
- The quasiideal construction, particularly Appendix F, is the source of the clean dimension advantage used here. The current work does not claim a new ideal-clock accuracy theorem.

## Yang2020

Yuxiang Yang and Renato Renner, **“Ultimate limit on time signal generation,”** arXiv:2004.07857v1 (2020).

- [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.07857v1).
- Dimension-dependent limits on the concentration of generated time signals. Care is required when comparing these one-signal statements to stationary signed counts or correlated nonrenewal outputs.

## Dost2023

Arman Pour Tak Dost and Mischa P. Woods, **“Quantum advantages in timekeeping: dimensional advantage, entropic advantage and how to realise them via Berry phases and ultra-regular spontaneous emission,”** arXiv:2303.10029v1 (2023).

- [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10029v1); [full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.10029v1).
- Relevant locations: the entropy discussion in Section IV and the multiple-tick reset proposal in Appendix C.

## Meier2025

Florian Meier, Yuri Minoguchi, Simon Sundelin, Tony J. G. Apollaro, Paul Erker, Simone Gasparinetti, and Marcus Huber, **“Precision is not limited by the second law of thermodynamics,”** *Nature Physics* **21**, 1147–1152 (2025).

- [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-025-02929-2).
- [Published full text with Methods](https://research.chalmers.se/publication/546881/file/546881_Fulltext.pdf).
- [arXiv:2407.07948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07948).
- The closest architectural antecedent. Relevant Methods sections: “Theory of the precision–entropy scaling” and “Applicability of the master equation.”

## Schwarzhans2021

Emanuel Schwarzhans, Maximilian P. E. Lock, Paul Erker, Nicolai Friis, and Marcus Huber, **“Autonomous Temporal Probability Concentration: Clockworks and the Second Law of Thermodynamics,”** *Physical Review X* **11**, 011046 (2021).

- [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.11.011046).
- [arXiv:2007.01307v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.01307v2); [full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.01307v2), dated 17 March 2021.
- Relevant locations: Section II's thermal-machine preparation, Section III's irreversible tick model, and Sections IV–V's complexity and finite-decay tradeoffs.

## Trushechkin2021

- [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.103.062226).

## Gerry2023

## Luo2026

## Prech2025

Kacper Prech, Gabriel T. Landi, Florian Meier, Nuriya Nurgalieva, Patrick P. Potts, Ralph Silva, and Mark T. Mitchison, **“Optimal Time Estimation and the Clock Uncertainty Relation for Stochastic Processes,”** *Physical Review X* **15**, 031068 (2025).

- [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1103/rpls-mp8z).
- Classical time estimation from observed Markov trajectories and the appropriate distinction between a chosen tick statistic and all information in an untimed event record.

## Meier2026

Florian Meier, Yuri Minoguchi, Gianmichele Blasi, Géraldine Haack, and Marcus Huber, **“Exponential gain in clock precision using quantum correlated ticks,”** arXiv:2601.10785v1 (2026).

- [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10785v1); [HTML full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10785v1).
- Autonomous correlations between consecutive ticks provide a mechanism distinct from complete-reset waiting-time regularity.

## Nelmes2026

Chad Nelmes, Emanuel Schwarzhans, Tony Apollaro, Timothy Spiller, and Irene D'Amico, **“Approaching the Limit of Quantum Clock Precision,”** arXiv:2604.22704v1 (2026).

- [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22704v1).
- [Institutional full text](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/241620/1/2604.22704v1.pdf).

## Wadhia2025

## Schwarzhans2026

Emanuel Schwarzhans, Tony J. G. Apollaro, Ilia Khomchenko, Maximilian P. E. Lock, Mark T. Mitchison, and Marcus Huber, **“Quantum Detectors as Autonomous Machines: Assessing the Nonequilibrium Thermodynamics of Information Acquisition,”** *PRX Quantum* **7**, 033001 (2026).

- [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1103/wm5p-tjtg).
- [arXiv:2508.16375v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16375v1); [HTML full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.16375v1).

## Gong2026

Longyan Gong, **“Comment on ‘Entropic Costs of Extracting Classical Ticks from a Quantum Clock’,”** arXiv:2605.21833v1 (2026).

- [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21833v1).
- A critical comment on interpreting a particular readout apparatus's dissipation as a fundamental timekeeping cost. It is cited as part of the discussion, not as an established universal bound.

## WoodsHorodecki2023

Mischa P. Woods and Michał Horodecki, **“Autonomous Quantum Devices: When Are They Realizable without Additional Thermodynamic Costs?”** *Physical Review X* **13**, 011016 (2023).

- [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.13.011016).
- [arXiv:1912.05562](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.05562).
- Autonomous implementation of thermodynamic operations using an explicit control clock, including bounds on its backreaction.

## Burgarth2023

Daniel Burgarth, Paolo Facchi, and Robin Hillier, **“Control of Quantum Noise: On the Role of Dilations,”** *Annales Henri Poincaré* **24**, 325–347 (2023); published online 24 July 2022.

- [Published full text](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00023-022-01211-y).
